Why people switch

Two years of Paste's annual plan comes to $59.98. Cutling is {{CUTLING_PRICE_ONCE}}, once, for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

The snippets you keep on a Pinboard are the ones Cutling is made for. Each cutling has a name, one of 12 colours and an icon, so you find it on the keyboard at a glance. In an email field your email cutlings come first. Image cutlings hold a payment QR code or a scan of your signature, and a temporary cutling can delete itself on a date you pick.

On iPhone and iPad, Cutling never watches what you copy. On a Mac it keeps a history you open with ⌘⇧V, and that history skips anything a password manager marks as concealed. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected: no account, no analytics and no server of Cutling's own.

Which one fits

If you want every copy on every device in one searchable history, with shared Pinboards for a team, Paste is made for that, and its subscription pays for it. If what you need is the same few things typed for you, everywhere, without a subscription, that is Cutling.

Other Paste alternatives you pay for once

  • PastePal is free to download, and its $7.99 Pro purchase unlocks pasting. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad, it keeps your history in step through iCloud, and it has an iPhone keyboard. It keeps your whole history in step, like Paste, for a one-time price.
  • ClipBook is $14.99 for one Mac, $24.99 for two or $49.99 for five, after a 5-day trial. It is Mac only: its own comparison with Paste lists no iPhone app and no iCloud sync.
  • Maccy is free and open source, or $9.99 on the Mac App Store if you want to support its developer. It is Mac only, and the history stays on that Mac.

Moving your Pinboards over

The snippets on your Paste Pinboards are what Cutling is for. Copy one, tap Cutling in Control Center to start a cutling from what you copied, give it a name, and it is in the keyboard. Text from Mail keeps its bold, italics and links.
