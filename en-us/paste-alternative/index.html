A Paste alternative you pay for once

Prices and features read from each app's App Store page and website on 20 November 2026.

Paste keeps everything you copy on your Mac, iPhone and iPad, and costs $3.99 a month, $29.99 a year or $89.99 for life. Cutling is $4.99, paid once. It keeps the things you choose to save, like an address, an account number or a reply you send every day, in an iPhone keyboard and behind one shortcut on a Mac. If you want every copy on every device and searchable, Paste does that and Cutling doesn't.

Where Paste is better

Paste is built to remember. It saves everything you copy without being asked, on every device, and its search reads the words inside screenshots and photos. If you often think "I copied that last week", Cutling won't find it on your iPhone, because on iPhone and iPad it holds only what you saved.

Paste also has Pinboards you can share with view or edit rights, a Team Plan sold on its website, Paste Stack for pasting several items in order, and on the Mac a connection to AI assistants such as Claude and Cursor through MCP, according to its listing. Cutling has none of these.

And if what you want is Paste without the subscription, Paste sells that too. Its Lifetime plan is $89.99 and covers Mac, iPhone and iPad.

Where Cutling fits

You type the same few things again and again: a delivery address, the account number people pay you into, an email signature, the answer to a question customers ask every day. You want them in a keyboard, one tap away in Messages, Mail or a shop's checkout, and you don't want another subscription.

Two years of Paste's annual plan comes to $59.98. Cutling is $4.99, paid once, and the Mac app comes with it.

On iPhone and iPad, Cutling doesn't watch what you copy. On a Mac it keeps a history you open with ⌘⇧V, and that history skips anything a password manager marks as concealed, so a copied password never lands in it. The App Store privacy label says Data Not Collected: there is no account, no analytics and no server of Cutling's own.

Image cutlings hold things like a payment QR code or a scan of your signature, and a temporary cutling can be given a date to delete itself.

Other Paste alternatives you pay for once

  • PastePal is free to download, and its $7.99 Pro purchase unlocks pasting. One purchase covers Mac, iPhone and iPad, it keeps your history in step through iCloud, and it has an iPhone keyboard. If you want Paste's whole-history sync and pay once, look here first.
  • ClipBook is $14.99 for one Mac, $24.99 for two or $49.99 for five, after a 5-day trial. It is Mac only: its own comparison with Paste lists no iPhone app and no iCloud sync.
  • Maccy is free and open source, or $9.99 on the Mac App Store if you want to support its developer. It is Mac only, and the history stays on that Mac.

Moving your Pinboards over

The snippets on your Paste Pinboards are what Cutling is for. Copy one, tap Cutling in Control Center to start a cutling from what you copied, give it a name, and it is in the keyboard. Text from Mail keeps its bold, italics and links.
