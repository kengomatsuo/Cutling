# Download Cutling

One app, a native experience on every Apple device.

## iPhone & iPad

Save snippets and images and insert them from any app with the custom keyboard.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6759476314?pt=127609326&ct=llms&mt=8)

Requires iOS 18 or later.

## Mac

Menu-bar app with a global hotkey and optional direct paste. Get it on the Mac App Store, or as a free direct download signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

[Download on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6759476314?pt=127609326&ct=llms&mt=12&platform=mac) [Download Directly (Free)](https://github.com/kengomatsuo/Cutling/releases/latest)

On the Mac App Store, or free directly from this site. Requires macOS 15 or later. Universal (Apple silicon & Intel).

## Installing on Mac

From the Mac App Store, Cutling installs like any other app. For the direct download, open the DMG and drag Cutling into your Applications folder. Because the app is notarized by Apple, it opens normally with no security warnings.
