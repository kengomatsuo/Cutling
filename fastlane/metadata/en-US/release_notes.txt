Blanks and dates in your cutlings. Write {Name} where a name goes and {date} for today's date. The Cutling keyboard types up to each blank and waits while you fill it with your usual keyboard, or with one tap on a value you used before. {Plan: Basic | Pro} gives you choices, and {cursor} sets where the cursor lands. The editor's Add Fill-in or Date menu puts them in for you.

Room for 1,000 text cutlings and 50 images.
