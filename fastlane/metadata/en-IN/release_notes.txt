Blanks and dates in your cutlings. Write {Name} where a name goes and {date} for today's date. Type the name first and tap the cutling, or tap it and fill the blank from the Cutling keyboard, with values you used before one tap away. {Plan: Basic | Pro} gives you choices, and {cursor} sets where the cursor lands. The editor's Add Fill-in or Date menu puts them in for you.

Room for 1,000 text cutlings and 50 images.
