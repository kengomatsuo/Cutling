Blanks and dates in your cutlings. In the editor, Add Fill-in or Date puts in a blank, such as a name or a choice of plans, or today's date. Type the name first and tap the cutling, or tap it and fill the blank from the Cutling keyboard, with values you used before one tap away. Braces you type yourself stay as text.
