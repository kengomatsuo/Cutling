## What the best sets share, and the rivals do not

- They show one idea per frame at poster scale. Paste's frame 1 has seven things in it; Opal's has two.
- The thing that crosses a seam is never a small tile: it is the biggest object in the strip
  (Flighty's aircraft and path, Structured's phone, Any Distance's route line).
- Colour is either fully saturated or fully dark. Nobody in this group uses a pale tint ground.
- A device is optional. Arc, Copilot, (Not Boring) and CARROT each spend a frame without one.

## Apple's limit that all three directions keep

"Depending on the orientation of your screenshots, the first one to three images will appear in
search results when no app preview is available." (Apple, Creating your product page.) Three
portrait frames are the most search shows, so each direction is one picture across frames 1 to 3
and each frame still stands alone on the product page.
