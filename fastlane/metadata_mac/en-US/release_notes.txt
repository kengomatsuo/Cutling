Blanks and dates in your cutlings. In the editor, Add Fill-in or Date puts in a blank, such as a name or a choice of plans, or today's date. Pick the cutling with ⌘⇧V and a short form asks for the blanks before it pastes, with values you used before one click away. Braces you type yourself stay as text.

Room for 5,000 text cutlings and 500 images, and up to 10,000 characters in each cutling.
