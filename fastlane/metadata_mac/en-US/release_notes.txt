Write a reply once and use it for everyone. A cutling can now hold a blank for the part that changes, such as a name or an order number. Pick the cutling with ⌘⇧V and a short form asks for the name before it pastes, with the ones you used before one click away.

Dates fill themselves in. Put today's date, tomorrow, a week from now or the time into a cutling and it is right on the day you use it. A cutling can also offer a choice (Basic or Pro, say) or take in the text you last copied. All of these are under Add Variable, right below the text when you edit a cutling. Braces you typed yourself stay as plain text, so the cutlings you already have paste as before.

There is far more room: 5,000 text cutlings and 500 images, up from 100 and 25. Each cutling can run to 10,000 characters, so a whole email fits.
