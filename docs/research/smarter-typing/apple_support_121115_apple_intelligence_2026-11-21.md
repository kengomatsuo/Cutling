Source: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/121115 (title "Get started with the next generation of Apple Intelligence", Published Date: November 08, 2026). Read in Chrome get_page_text 2026-11-21. Extract of the parts used; wording kept short.

Device requirements (iOS 27): iPhone Duo, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone Air; iPad mini (A17 Pro and A20 Pro), iPad models with M1 or later; Mac with M1 or later; others.
Storage: up to 14 GB for iPhone Duo, iPhone 17 Pro models and later, iPhone Air (and iPad M4+/Mac M3+ with 12 GB memory); up to 8 GB for other Apple Intelligence iPhones.
Device language and Siri language must be set to the same supported language.
Languages: English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Chinese (simplified), Chinese (traditional), Japanese, Korean. Some features not in all regions or languages.
China mainland: Apple Intelligence features not currently working on devices bought there, or used there with a mainland Apple Account.
Usage limits: features that rely on server-side models (Siri AI, photo tools, Image Playground, AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts) have daily limits.
Features listed that matter for typing: "Suggestions in Mail (English only)", "Personalised Smart Reply in Messages (English only)", "Personalised Smart Reply in Mail", "Automatic proofreading", "Describe a calendar event in natural language", "Describe a reminder in natural language", and (iOS 26 and later) "Writing Tools, available for users without Siri AI writing support".
Not in the language list: Indonesian, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Polish, Russian, Hebrew, Malay.
