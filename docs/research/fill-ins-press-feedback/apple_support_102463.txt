Change iPhone keyboard sounds or haptics. iOS 16 or later. Sounds & Haptics > Keyboard Feedback: Sound on/off; Haptic on/off. Turning on keyboard haptics might affect battery life. If you don't feel haptics, make sure Vibration is on in Settings > Accessibility > Touch.
