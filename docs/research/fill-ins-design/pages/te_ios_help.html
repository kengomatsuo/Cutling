TextExpander for iPhone & iPad

Overview

TextExpander for iPhone and iPad brings your Snippets to the places you type on mobile. Install the app from the App Store, sign in to your TextExpander account, and add the TextExpander keyboard to expand Snippets in apps like Messages, Mail, Slack, Notes, and more.

What you can do

  • Expand Snippets in apps where you type
  • Search and insert Snippets from the keyboard
  • Use Fill-in and macros on your mobile device
  • Swipe to type with the TextExpander keyboard
  • Search emoji without leaving the keyboard

Before you start

TextExpander for iPhone and iPad requires iOS 18 or later. You’ll also need a TextExpander account. If you don’t have one yet, create an account at textexpander.com before signing in to the iOS app.
