## Patterns that repeat

- The whole message stays visible and the blank sits inside the sentence (TextExpander iOS and Mac, Snippety Live Template, Espanso). Only Raycast (Mac and iOS) and ClipKeyboard use a separate field plus a preview.
- Cancel and the commit button sit together at the bottom right: Cancel / Expand, Cancel / Paste, Cancel / OK. Raycast iOS puts one commit pill, Insert, at the right of the strip and a back arrow at the left.
- Every product that takes typed text on iPhone draws its own letter keys in Apple's shape: 123, emoji, space bar, return, with globe and dictation under it (TextExpander, Snippety, Raycast). The fill-in card or strip replaces the prediction strip's place or floats over the top of the keys.
- Saved values as chips with a small label (ClipKeyboard) and one-key-per-value rows (WordBoard) avoid typing at all.
- A coloured pill marks a blank, with colour by type (Snippety: blue text, pink variable, dark date); a popup chevron marks a choice.

## What Apple's keyboard does that a fill-in screen could copy

- One strip at the top, hairline-divided, holding at most three items: it is the place for the next-blank hint, a saved value, or the clipboard. (Predictive bar figure.)
- Keys and the bottom row never move: the globe stays at the left under the keys, the emoji keyboard keeps "ABC" at the bottom left as the one way back and delete at the right. A fill-in mode could keep that row as it is and put "Back" in the strip. (Emoji keyboard and picker figures.)
- The return key changes its label to match the task (HIG: Return key type). In a fill-in screen it could read Next on every blank but the last, and Insert on the last.
- The emoji keyboard puts a search field in the strip's place rather than a floating card. (GUESS: that reads as the same family as the Apple keyboard; the TextExpander card instead floats above and covers the host app.)
- The HIG's hidden-button figure warns that a control the keyboard covers must stay in view; a commit button should sit at or above the strip.
- Dictation shows its state with one filled mic and the caret mark and leaves the keys alone.
- The dark theme is the same layout on dark-grey keys; nothing is rearranged.
