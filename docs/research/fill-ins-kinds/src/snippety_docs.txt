On this page
Snippety is an advanced snippet manager that allows you to create, organize, and
share snippets. It is designed to be fast, secure, configurable, and easy to use.
The app is focused to provide intuitive user experience, but also to give power
users the flexibility they need to manage their snippets effectively.
This guide will help you get started with Snippety and show you how to create
your first snippet.
Project Values​
Simplicity: Snippety is designed to be simple and easy to use.
Flexibility: Snippety is designed to be flexible and configurable.
User Focus: Snippety is designed with the user in mind. Many features are
implemented based on user feedback. Every e-mail is read and every feature
request is considered.
Privacy: Snippety is designed to respect your privacy. Your data is
stored locally on your device and is never shared with anyone. The only things
that are collected are crash reports and analytics data, which are anonymized,
do not contain any of your input (like search phrases), and are necessary
to improve & maintain the app.
Security: Most applications store your data on their servers, which can
be a security risk. Snippety stores your data locally on your device and
optionally syncs it securely between your devices using Apple's iCloud servers
within your account, avoiding any third-party servers.
