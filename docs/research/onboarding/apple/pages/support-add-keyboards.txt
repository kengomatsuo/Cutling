Add or change keyboards on iPhone
You can add keyboards for writing or using Dictation in different languages on your iPhone. You can also change the layout of your onscreen or external keyboard.
For some supported languages, you can type in two languages without having to switch between keyboards. Your keyboard automatically switches between the two languages you use most often. See the iOS and iPadOS Feature Availability webpage.
Add or remove a keyboard for another language
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap General, then tap Keyboard.
Tap Keyboards, then do any of the following:
Add a keyboard: Tap Add New Keyboard, then choose a keyboard from the list. Repeat to add more keyboards.
Use a bilingual keyboard: For languages such as English (U.S.) and Spanish (Latin America), tap Add New Keyboard, then tap Add to [language] Keyboard. (Available for select language pairs.)
Remove a keyboard: Swipe left on the keyboard, then tap Delete.
Reorder your keyboard list: Tap Edit, drag  next to a keyboard to a new place in the list, then tap .
If you add a keyboard for a different language, the corresponding language is automatically added to the Preferred Languages list. To view this list and add languages to it directly, go to the Settings app, tap General, then tap Language & Region. To change how apps and websites display text, reorder the Preferred Languages list.
Switch to another keyboard
While typing text, touch and hold  or .
Tap the keyboard you want to switch to.
You can also switch between Magic Keyboard and other keyboards.
Assign an alternative layout to a keyboard
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap General, tap Keyboard, then tap Keyboards.
Tap a language, then select an alternative layout from the list.
