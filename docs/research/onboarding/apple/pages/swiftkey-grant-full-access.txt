How do I grant full access to the Microsoft SwiftKey Keyboard in iOS?
There are a few instances where Microsoft SwiftKey will need to be given full access again, even if it had previously been given full access and was working fine. When this happens, you will see a message above your keyboard telling you that you need to enable full access. You will need to toggle full access off and on to reset this and fix the problem.
Steps: Go to Apple Settings, and select General. Tap Keyboard and then Keyboards. Tap Microsoft SwiftKey, and then toggle the Allow Full Access switch. Press Allow.
