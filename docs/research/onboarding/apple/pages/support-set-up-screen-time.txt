Set up Screen Time for yourself on iPhone
With Screen Time, you can get information about how much time you spend on your devices—including which apps and websites you use and for how long. You can use Time Allowances to balance time across entertainment, games, and social media apps. You can also set schedules to manage when those apps are available, and content restrictions to manage what can be accessed.
Note: To use all the latest Screen Time features, you need iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later on all your Apple devices. If you’re setting up these features in a Family Sharing group, all devices must be upgraded to iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later. Learn how to update your Apple devices.
If you want to set up Screen Time or parental control features for your child, see Apple Devices for Kids and Families.
Turn on Screen Time
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap Screen Time, then do any of the following:
Tap Screen Time Schedule, then tap Turn On App & Website Activity.
Tap Time Allowances, then tap Turn On App & Website Activity.
Tap Manage Screen Time, then tap Turn On App & Website Activity.
Note: You can quickly check your device use by adding a Screen Time widget to your Home Screen.
Use Screen Time across your devices
You can sync your Screen Time settings and reports across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac where you’re signed in to the same Apple Account. This means your Screen Time Schedule, Time Allowances, and usage data are shared across your devices. You can also see your combined Screen Time activity across devices.
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap Screen Time, tap Manage Screen Time, then turn on Share Across Devices.
When Share Across Devices is on, any Screen Time limits or schedules you set apply to your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. If you turn off Share Across Devices, each device has its own Screen Time settings.
Note: To use Share Across Devices, you need iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later on all your Apple devices. If you’re setting up these features in a Family Sharing group, all devices must be upgraded to iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27, or later. Learn how to update your Apple devices.
View Screen Time summaries
After you turn on Screen Time, you can get a weekly report with an overview of the time you spend on your devices, your most used apps, how often you pick up your iPhone and other devices, and more.
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap Screen Time, then tap Daily Average.
Tap Week to show a summary of weekly use, or tap Day to show a summary of daily use.
You can also scroll down to view a report of your Pickups and Notifications to see how many times you picked up your devices and how many notifications you received. You can swipe left and right on your summaries to view your history.
Turn off Screen Time
Go to the Settings app  on your iPhone.
Tap Screen Time, then do any of the following:
Turn off Screen Time: Tap Manage Screen Time, then tap Turn Off App & Website Activity.
Turn off your Screen Time schedule: Tap Screen Time Schedule, then turn off Schedule.
Turn off your Time Allowances for apps: Tap Time Allowances, tap Manage Categories, select a category, then select Delete (your category name) Category.
You can also view your summary by tapping a Screen Time Weekly Report notification when one appears on your screen. (If the notification disappears, you can find it in Notification Center.)
