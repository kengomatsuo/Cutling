## Resolution

Done on the user's **iPhone 14 Pro running iOS 27.0.1**. The test app and keyboard (Full Access off, `PrimaryLanguage = fr-FR-x-kabuki`) are on the throwaway branch [`task/on-device-checks`](https://github.com/jmlivingston/keyboard-kabuki/tree/task/on-device-checks). Screenshots were shared in the session.

**Signing and App Group**
- ✅ The user is on the **paid Apple Developer Program** (expires April 2027), not a free account. Builds last until the membership expires, so the **7-day reinstall** and **3-apps-per-device** checks no longer apply and were dropped. The App Group had to be ticked once per target in Xcode's Signing & Capabilities. `xcodebuild -allowProvisioningUpdates` won't register it on its own. The phone needed pairing and Developer Mode.
- ✅ **The keyboard reads the App Group with Full Access off.** It showed "App Group defaults: ok, container: ok" and read the tint the app wrote (#FFC2C2).
- ✅ **Tint changes show up live while the keyboard is open** (polling the App Group once a second; "changes seen while open: 2"), and also on the next appearance. This is better than the "next time it appears" assumed in the Language Tint screen decision.

**Haptics and click**
- ❌ **No haptics and no input click** from the keyboard with Full Access off: `UIImpactFeedbackGenerator`, `UISelectionFeedbackGenerator`, `UINotificationFeedbackGenerator` and `playInputClick` were all silent, even with Settings › Sounds & Haptics › Keyboard Feedback › Haptic and Sound on. The keyboard's buttons did work (it typed "a"), and Apple's keyboard gives both on the same phone.

**Detecting and enabling the keyboard**
- `activeInputModes` lists our keyboard, but its `primaryLanguage` comes back as **`fr-FR`**: the `-x-kabuki` tag is dropped. Settings shows it as "Kabuki Diagnostic — App / French". **A custom language tag can't tell our keyboard apart from Apple's French.**
- ✅ `openSettingsURLString` opens the app's own Settings page, which has a **Keyboards** row (Siri, Search, Cellular Data, Keyboards). Inside it is a **toggle for the keyboard**, and **no "Allow Full Access" toggle** because we don't request it. So the app can send users one button + two taps from turning the keyboard on.
- iOS shows "When using one of these keyboards, the keyboard can access all the data you type" on both Keyboards screens, even with Full Access off.

**Apple's French (France) keyboard on iOS 27.0.1**
- **Return key:** glyphs, not words. Default shows ⏎, search a blue 🔍, send a blue ↑.
- **123 page:** `1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0` / `- / : ; ( ) $ & @ "` / `#+= . , ? ! ’ ⌫` / `ABC · space (FR) · ⏎`. The currency key is $, likely following the US region.
- **#+= page:** `[ ] { } # % ^ * + =` / `_ \ | ~ < > € £ ¥ •` / `123 . , ? ! ’ ⌫` / `ABC · space · ⏎`. It's identical to the English & Spanish page.
- **The key after N depends on what's been typed:** it shows **´** at the start of a word (long-press `¨ ^ ` ´`) and **’** right after a letter. Tapping ´ inserts a standalone `´`; it is **not a dead key**: ´ then e gives `´e`, not é.
- **Long-press o** opens **to the left**, reading `º ō ø õ ó ò œ ô o ö` left to right with plain o next to the key. That's a different order from the research data.
- Apple shows a prediction bar (Je / Tu / C'est). Ours won't, per the privacy rule.
- **Not captured:** long-press pop-ups on the 123 page, and the u/n pop-ups.
