## Measured layout facts (from screenshots; GUESS = approximate from a 490 px wide phone frame, about 1.1 px per point)

Journal welcome (shots/02-apple-journal-welcome.png), Apple's own:
- Presented as a sheet: the previous screen shows as a card edge above it, rounded top corners, no grabber, no close button, no toolbar.
- Order top to bottom: app icon (about 120 px wide, GUESS 110 pt, rounded square, app's real icon), title in two lines left aligned ("Welcome to" in the app tint purple, "Journal" in label colour, both bold, GUESS 28-30 pt), three rows, a large empty gap, Continue button.
- Rows: SF Symbol in the app tint at left (about 40 px wide column), body text in label colour regular at about 17 pt, two to three lines each, row pitch about 95-100 px (GUESS 45 pt gap between rows). Text is full black, not secondary grey, and there is no bold row title: one sentence per row.
- Button: full width minus about 16 pt sides, about 58 px tall (GUESS 50 pt), fully rounded capsule, filled with the app tint, white semibold label, about 24 px above the home indicator.
- Three rows, no scroll visible at this size.
Apple Sports (shots/04-apple-sports-get-started.png): no sheet; white capsule "Get Started", black label, centred under a centred one-line caption on the live screen.
WhatsNewKit's look-alike (shots/01-whatsnewkit-sheet.png): title centred, rows titled in bold with grey subtitle, icon column 40 pt; this differs from Journal's left-aligned two-line title and single-sentence rows: UNSURE which Apple's iWork What's New sheet uses, nobody here has a picture of it.
