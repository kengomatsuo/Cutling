 Things for iPad 3.23
 August 19, 2026 
 Requires iPadOS 16.4 or later
 Added support for completing a repeating to-do early: it now shows a normal checkbox, just like any other to-do.
 Added support for rescheduling a repeating to-do – just bump it to a new date:
 If it repeats on a fixed schedule, Things asks whether to make a one-time exception, or update the rule going forward.
 If it repeats after completion, it simply moves to the new date.
 Made a number of other refinements to repeating to-dos:
 Refreshed the look of the setup dialog, which now makes it easier to pick the next recurrence date.
 Added a small gray repeat icon to a copy of a repeating to-do, to distinguish it from a regular one.
 Added support for starting a repeating project early: as soon as you edit its to-dos, Things offers to create the next copy of the project ahead of time.
 Added support for creating the next copy of a repeating to-do ahead of time: expand the to-do, tap its repeat rule, then tap Create Next Copy .
 Added support for pausing, resuming, and stopping multiple repeating to-dos at once.
 Unified the display and editing of to-dos – both in lists and when expanded – so Mac and iOS now share the same code.
 Repeating templates now appear in the Deadlines list (search for “deadlines” to view this list).
 The Repeat command is now available in the Items menu.
 You can now set a “Start Early” value of up to 100 days for an after-completion repeating to-do.
 Fixed a bug where a repeating to-do shown in Upcoming for a given date might not actually be created on that date.
 Fixed a bug where an after-completion repeating to-do could appear in the wrong place in Upcoming, or show as “Waiting” when it should have been scheduled.
 Fixed a bug where the Starts and Ends fields in the Repeat dialog could use different date formats.
 Fixed a bug where repeating templates couldn’t be reordered in area lists.
 Fixed a bug where you couldn’t create a to-do in a tag list if that tag had children.
 Fixed a bug where enabling Mail to Things in Settings didn’t work.
 Fixed a bug that allowed unreasonable “Start Early” values for after-completion repeating to-dos when set to a frequency other than days.
 Fixed several localization issues in the description of a repeating to-do’s recurrence rule.
 Things now requires iPadOS 16.4 or later.

 Things for iPad 3.7
 September 17, 2018 
 Requires iOS 10 or later
 SIRI SHORTCUTS: On iOS 12, you can use the new Siri Shortcuts feature. Siri will determine common actions you perform inside of Things and suggest them as shortcuts on your Lock screen, like “Show Today” or “Add To-Do”. But you can also build your own shortcuts in Things’ settings and record a voice trigger for them. Here are a few examples of what shortcuts can do:
 Build your own Quick Entry – Record shortcuts for different things you enter often, like Errands or Movies you want to watch. Every time you invoke these shortcuts (e.g. “Hey Siri, add an errand”), Things opens to reveal a new to-do, with all the relevant details pre-filled, like tags, the list it should go to, etc. – all that’s left for you to do is enter the title and hit save.
 Ready-made to-dos – Create shortcuts for adding to-dos that are always exactly the same, like a packing list, or your favorite recipe. When you invoke the shortcut, Things adds it in the background.
 Quick access to lists – Record shortcuts to open particular lists, like Today, Upcoming, a project you’re working on, your Work area, and so on. You can also specify a tag that the list should be filtered by when it opens.
 Build Workflows – Any shortcut can also be used in Apple’s new Shortcuts app, which allows you to combine multiple shortcuts from different apps into one workflow.
 DYNAMIC NOTIFICATIONS: When you get a notification, tap the Snooze button and you’ll notice that you now have granular control over the duration of the snooze: 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour.
 PASSWORD AUTOFILL: Things supports iOS 12’s new Password AutoFill feature. It creates a strong password for you, keeps it safe, and automatically enters it for you when you log in on a new device. All of this now works with Things Cloud.

 Things for iPad 3.0.0
 May 18, 2017 
 Requires iOS 10.0 or later
 The first release of Things 3 for iPad, including these headline features:
 ALL-NEW DESIGN: How it looks, how it works, and how it feels – Things has been completely redesigned, with more clarity, improved workflows, better structure, and delightful animations. It’s a to-do list like no other.
 PROJECT HEADINGS: It’s so much easier to handle your projects now that you can break them up with headings. Use them to create categories, milestones, or whatever you need – just give each one a name and drag your to-dos underneath. Instantly you’ve got a nice, clean structure for your list, and the plan becomes perfectly clear.
 CHECKLISTS: Some things take several steps to complete but don’t require a full-blown project. Now you can use Checklists to break down the finer details of a to-do. Use them for shopping lists, when packing your bag for a trip, or to detail a process at work so you won’t miss a critical step.
 DESKTOP-CLASS LIST EDITING: Editing your lists on iOS now comes with lots of power, and no friction. Swipe to-dos to multi-select them, then edit them all at once: set their date, move them to another list, or tap and drag the group together.
 MAGIC PLUS BUTTON: Finally, a versatile way to insert a to-do right where you want it! Just drag the magical button with your finger and let go. You can also drag it toward the left margin in a project to create a Heading, or drop a new item to your inbox from any other list.
 CALENDAR INTEGRATION: Display calendar events alongside your to-dos and stay on top of your schedule. They will show at the top of your Today list, and for each day in Upcoming, where you plan the week ahead.
 JUMP START: Working with dates is super easy with the new Jump Start popover. Simply swipe on any to-do to bring it up, then quickly choose when you want to start: Today, a later date, or put it on hold in Someday.
 NATURAL LANGUAGE RECOGNITION: The Jump Start popover has a magical ingredient: Natural Language Recognition. It smartly detects what you’re typing to work out what you mean... “Tom(orrow)”, “Sat(urday)”, “in fou(r days)”, “Au(gust 1)”. As you can see, it jumps to all the right conclusions.
 PLAN YOUR DAY – WITH TODAY AND THIS EVENING: Once you’ve made your plan, the Today list is your go-to place for all daily activities. Calendar events now display at the top, giving an outline of your schedule. Your to-dos follow, with a discrete section at the bottom called This Evening for things you’ll get to later in the day.
 PLAN YOUR WEEK – WITH UPCOMING: Plan your week ahead with the new Upcoming list. It shows everything on your agenda for the coming days: scheduled to-dos, repeating to-dos, deadlines, and calendar events. A quick peek at this list is all it takes to stay on top of your schedule.
 REMINDERS: One of Things’ most-requested features is now here: time-based reminders! You can add a reminder by typing (natural language recognition), or just speak to Siri. For those to-dos you absolutely cannot miss, reminders will give you peace of mind.
 QUICK FIND: Search and navigation in Things is now extremely fast, with Quick Find. All you need to do is swipe down in any list and start typing – the name of a project, to-do, or tag – and instantly you’re taken there. It’s magic!
 APP-WIDE TAG FILTERS: Just search for the name of any tag to pull up an app-wide filter. It shows all items with that tag across all your projects and areas. It’s a fantastically versatile way to view your to-dos.
 THE NEW SOURCE LIST: Projects in the source list are now nested under areas to keep everything tidy. Each one has a new progress indicator to show you how close you are to achieving your goal.
 And much more... see what’s new .
