 2024.2 (22 January 2024)
 New 
 Introducing Shared Journals! Share daily highlights, life updates, photos, and more in a shared space, fostering deeper connections with friends or family.
 Separation of Journals: Your personal and Shared Journals are always kept separate, providing you with the flexibility and peace of mind to choose between sharing your journaling experience or keeping it private. Whether you opt for a shared or personal journal, your content is always secure and distinct.
 Security and Privacy in Shared Journals: Shared Journals are powered by the same robust end-to-end encryption technology as your personal Day One journals. This ensures that your shared space remains a safe space, with content visible only to those you invite.
 Unlimited Creation for Premium Users: Premium users can create an unlimited number of Shared Journals. Invite anyone to view, comment, or contribute to your Shared Journals. Non-premium users can access your Shared Journals at no cost.
 Updated 
 We’ve made several updates and enhancements to our existing features to further enrich your journaling experience
 We’ve ensured a default personal journal is always available
 Updated options are now more visible in the journal advanced settings

 We’re thrilled to offer the addition of Shared Journals in this release. Thank you for being a part of the Day One community and for trusting us with your journaling journey.
