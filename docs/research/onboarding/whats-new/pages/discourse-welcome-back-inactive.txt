 Customizing "Welcome back!" for Inactive users 
 Product Support 
 emyoulation 
 (Brian McCullough)
 March 17, 2026, 4:13am
 1 
 We leverage Discourse for doing support of our open source genealogy software. A large percentage of those users are older and not technically adept as the newer generations who were indoctrinated with microcomputers during primary schooling.
 Discourse offers a “Welcome back!” banner to users who haven’t logged in for a while. But the rest of the screen is just as cluttered as what a regular (experienced) visitor has grown accustomed to navigating. So users (who are coming in already being frustrated by not having been able to figure something out in a software package) are thrust into another complex and unfamiliar interface.
 Perhaps there is a way to offer a less noisy custom screen to dormant accounts, like the new user is shown a streamlined “welcome screen” or “onboarding experience”?
