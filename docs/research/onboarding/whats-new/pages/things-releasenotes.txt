 Things for Mac 3.23
 August 21, 2026 
 Requires macOS 13.3 or later
 Added support for completing a repeating to-do early: it now shows a normal checkbox, just like any other to-do.
 Added support for rescheduling a repeating to-do – just bump it to a new date:
 If it repeats on a fixed schedule, Things asks whether to make a one-time exception, or update the rule going forward.
 If it repeats after completion, it simply moves to the new date.
 Made a number of other refinements to repeating to-dos:
 Refreshed the look of the setup dialog, which now makes it easier to pick the next recurrence date.
 Added a small gray repeat icon to a copy of a repeating to-do, to distinguish it from a regular one.
 Added support for starting a repeating project early: as soon as you edit its to-dos, Things offers to create the next copy of the project ahead of time.
 Added support for creating the next copy of a repeating to-do ahead of time: Control-click the to-do and choose Repeat → Create Next Copy.
 Added support for pausing, resuming, and stopping multiple repeating to-dos at once.
 Unified the display and editing of to-dos – both in lists and when expanded – so Mac and iOS now share the same code.
 The deadline in an expanded to-do is now a button with a menu instead of a regular field.
 The When dialog now shows “No Results” for searches with no match.
 Added haptic feedback from the Trackpad when completing a to-do or checklist item.
 Added the standard Cmd+: shortcut for Show Spelling and Grammar, and Cmd+; for Check Spelling.
 Fixed a bug where you couldn’t create a to-do in a tag list if that tag had children.
 Fixed a bug where a newly created to-do wasn’t selected after pasting text from another app.
 Fixed a bug where toolbar buttons could become misaligned after collapsing or expanding to-dos.
 Fixed a bug where the selection wasn’t cleared when switching between lists.
 Fixed a bug where you couldn’t always complete a checklist item.
 Fixed a bug where reverse-tabbing in Quick Entry could get stuck in the Move dialog when Full Keyboard Access was enabled.
 Fixed a bug where search results could show items with a strikethrough even though they weren’t canceled.
 Fixed a bug where the yellow search highlight could persist in other views after leaving search.
 Fixed a bug where the cursor wouldn’t move to the When field in Quick Entry when pressing Cmd+S.
 Fixed a bug where Cmd+Delete in a checklist item deleted the whole line instead of just from the start of the line to the cursor.
 Fixed a bug where Opt+Delete in tag text deleted a single character instead of a whole word.
 Fixed a bug where the wrong ••• menu could appear when using the tag filter bar.
 Fixed a bug where clicking to the left of a checklist would scroll it to the top.
 Fixed a bug where clicking in a project’s notes would scroll the project to the top.
 Fixed a bug with dragging and dropping external content into the Inbox.
 Fixed a crash that could occur when quickly pasting text into a checklist.
 Fixed a crash that could occur when removing a project’s Today assignment while the When dialog was open.
 Things now requires macOS 13.3 or later.

 Things for Mac 3.21
 September 16, 2024 
 Requires macOS 10.15 or later
 Full compatibility with macOS 15 Sequoia.
 Added support for Apple Intelligence’s new Writing Tools. This feature will not be available until Apple Intelligence rolls out in the coming months (and won’t be available in all regions or on all devices). See Apple’s website for the latest information.
 Adjusted the app to work well with Sequoia’s new Window Tiling feature. Things’ windows will stay constrained to your chosen tile size, even if you’ve disabled “Preserve window width when resizing sidebar” in Things’ settings.
 Tweaked the widget’s + button for clearer distinction of where it will save to if your desktop’s appearance is set to Monochrome.
 Added support for hitting Shift Return in text to insert a “line separator” character (like Ctrl Return on macOS versions earlier than Sequoia). This allows you to hit return in a bulleted list for a new line, without creating a new bullet.
 Added support for the Shift Cmd T shortcut when the cursor is in an area’s title.
 Added the Tags command when Control-clicking an area heading in the Anytime and Someday lists (also in Today when grouping is enabled in Things’ settings).
 Fixed a crash that could occur when pasting an empty string into a list.
 Fixed some issues that could cause Things Helper to crash.

 Things for Mac 3.0.0
 May 18, 2017 
 Requires macOS 10.11 or later
 The first release of Things 3 for Mac, including these headline features:
 ALL-NEW DESIGN: How it looks, how it works, and how it feels – Things has been completely redesigned, with more clarity, improved workflows, better structure, and delightful animations. It’s a to-do list like no other.
 PROJECT HEADINGS: It’s so much easier to handle your projects now that you can break them up with headings. Use them to create categories, milestones, or whatever you need – just give each one a name and drag your to-dos underneath. Instantly you’ve got a nice, clean structure for your list, and the plan becomes perfectly clear.
 CHECKLISTS: Some things take several steps to complete but don’t require a full-blown project. Now you can use Checklists to break down the finer details of a to-do. Use them for shopping lists, when packing your bag for a trip, or to detail a process at work so you won’t miss a critical step.
 CALENDAR INTEGRATION: Display calendar events alongside your to-dos and stay on top of your schedule. They will show at the top of your Today list, and for each day in Upcoming, where you plan the week ahead.
 PLAN YOUR DAY – WITH TODAY AND THIS EVENING: Once you’ve made your plan, the Today list is your go-to place for all daily activities. Calendar events now display at the top, giving an outline of your schedule. Your to-dos follow, with a discrete section at the bottom called This Evening for things you’ll get to later in the day.
 PLAN YOUR WEEK – WITH UPCOMING: Plan your week ahead with the new Upcoming list. It shows everything on your agenda for the coming days: scheduled to-dos, repeating to-dos, deadlines, and calendar events. A quick peek at this list is all it takes to stay on top of your schedule.
 JUMP START: Working with dates is super easy with the new Jump Start popover. Use it to quickly put to-dos in your Today list, schedule them for later, or put them on hold in Someday.
 NATURAL LANGUAGE RECOGNITION: The When Selector has a magical ingredient: Natural Language Recognition. It smartly detects what you’re typing to work out what you mean... “Tom(orrow)”, “Sat(urday)”, “in fou(r days)”, “Au(gust 1)”. As you can see, it jumps to all the right conclusions.
 REMINDERS: One of Things’ most-requested features is now here: time-based reminders! You can add a reminder by typing (natural language recognition), or just speak to Siri. For those to-dos you absolutely cannot miss, reminders will give you peace of mind.
 TYPE TRAVEL: Search and navigation in Things is now extremely fast, with Type Travel. All you need to do is start typing – the name of a list, to-do, or tag – and instantly you’re taken there. It’s magic!
 APP-WIDE TAG FILTERS: Just start typing the name of any tag to pull up an app-wide filter. It shows all items with that tag across all your projects and areas. It’s a fantastically versatile way to view your to-dos.
 THE NEW SIDEBAR: Projects in the sidebar are now nested under areas to keep everything tidy. Each one has a new progress indicator to show you how close you are to achieving your goal.
 SLIM MODE: Collapse your sidebar with a two-finger swipe to cut out distractions and focus on your work. This is great when you’re working in Split View, or if you just need more room on your screen.
 MULTIPLE WINDOWS: You can now open multiple projects in their own windows and drag to-dos back and forth between them to organize with ease. Or use them across multiple displays, desktop spaces, or split views – whatever suits your personal workflow.
 And much more... see what’s new .
