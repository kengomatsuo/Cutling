      Powerful Features
Snippety is a feature-rich text expander designed to save you time every day.
      Click below to learn more.
          Conditional Blocks
          One snippet, many outcomes. Show a priority line for Premium customers and a
            standard one for everyone else, decided as the snippet expands.
          Smart Placeholders
          20 built-in placeholders: fill-in fields, dropdowns, calculated dates, clipboard and
            file contents, references to other snippets, all filled in as you expand.
          Per-App Snippets
          App Rules show only your code snippets in your IDE and only your email ones in Mail,
            so the right library is always in front of you.
          Syntax Highlighting
          Store code the way you write it, highlighted across 50+ languages. The only expander
            in this comparison that does.
          Real CLI
          A snippety command that expands, searches, creates, and edits from the
            terminal. Pipe a snippet into a commit message, or bind one to any hotkey.
          Shortcuts & Siri
          20+ native Shortcuts actions, so your snippets can trigger, and be triggered by,
            anything else on your Mac. Run them from Spotlight, the menu bar, or Siri.
          Scripts & Automation
          Run a shell script mid-snippet and drop its output straight in. Format the selected
            JSON with jq, pull data from an API, or simulate keystrokes to fill a form.
          Like Spotlight
          Works in every text field. Press ⌘⇧Space and access your snippets from anywhere.
          iCloud Sync
          Your library follows you to every Apple device through your own iCloud, with no
            third-party server in the middle.
          Collaboration
          Share snippets with your team or friends securely via iCloud. All of this with no
            subscriptions or hidden fees!
          Text Formatting
          Write in HTML, RTF, or Markdown and preview it before it lands, so formatted email
            replies stay formatted.
          250 Themes
          Pick what best fits your style and tweak it if needed. Choose from 250 built-in
            themes.
