Important pricing note for international customers

The prices shown above are in USD and are based on US tax regulations, which means they exclude tax. For some customers outside the US, this US base price may still apply if your country does not require tax-inclusive pricing.

If you’re located in a country where VAT or GST is required (including the European Union, United Kingdom, Australia, and others), your base price may include an estimated 20% increase to reflect local tax requirements.

When you check out, your final price will be converted to your local currency using exchange rates provided by our payment partner, Paddle. These rates may not always match real-time market rates exactly, but they’ll be close.

Your total price — including any required taxes — will always be shown clearly before you complete your purchase.

Pricing questions, answered

Is TextExpander free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever and needs no credit card. It includes 5 active Snippets, 1 Snippet swap every 2 weeks, and sharing with up to 5 team members across 3 teams. New accounts also get a 30-day preview of paid features.

How much does TextExpander cost?

Pro is $5 per user per month, billed annually ($60 per year). Business is $10 per user per month, billed annually ($120 per year). Monthly billing is also available; annual billing gives you two months free. Enterprise pricing is custom.

What happens when my 30-day preview ends?

Nothing you need to do. Your account moves to the Free plan automatically. All of your Snippets are kept, and 5 stay active. Upgrade any time to make them all active again.

What is a Snippet swap?

On the Free plan you can have 5 Snippets active at once, and you can swap one of them for a different Snippet every 2 weeks. Paid plans have no active-Snippet limit, so swaps don’t apply.

Do I need Business, or is Pro enough for my team?

Pro gives up to 5 users unlimited Snippets, unlimited teams, and dynamic content like date math and JavaScript. Choose Business for your whole team or organization: it removes the 5-user limit and adds Snippet Requests, guest sharing, Google SSO, and a longer Snippet activity history. Enterprise adds SSO for all providers with SCIM, granular sharing controls, custom reporting, invoiced billing, and a custom security review.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account settings at any time. Downgrading to Free keeps your Snippets. Annual plans run through the end of the paid term.

Does TextExpander work on Mac, Windows, Chrome, and mobile?

Yes. One account works across Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad, and the Chrome extension. Your Snippets sync everywhere on every plan, including Free.

Are prices in USD, and is tax included?

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. VAT or GST may be added at checkout depending on your country, and your total is shown before you pay. See the international pricing note on this page.
