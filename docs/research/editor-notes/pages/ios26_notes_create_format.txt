			Create and format notes on iPhone
Use the Notes app to jot down quick thoughts or organize detailed information with checklists, images, web links, scanned documents, handwritten notes, and sketches. You can even organize your note with sections and headings.
 [IMG: A note for planning a trip, with a calculation of the travel expenses and a photo.] Create a new note
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
Tap  [IMG: the New Note button] , then enter your text.
The first line of the note becomes the note’s title.
To save the note, tap  [IMG: the Done button] .
Add formatting, checklists, and tables
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
When writing a note, do any of the following:
Format text: Tap  [IMG: the Format button] , then choose a style such as Heading, bold or italic font, highlight, and more. You can also swipe over the format bar options to quickly access commonly used styles.
Make a checklist: Tap  [IMG: the Checklist button] .
Add a table: Tap  [IMG: the Add Table button] .
Organize a note with sections and headings
You can collapse and expand sections, making it easy to manage and outline long notes.
 [IMG: A note in the Notes app, with collapsible headings at the top of the screen.] Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
In a note, select the text for the heading.
Tap  [IMG: the Format button] , then tap Heading or Subheading.
Add content to the section, then do any of the following:
Collapse a section: Tap  [IMG: the Collapse button] .
Expand a section: Tap  [IMG: the Expand button] .
Use Writing Tools in Notes
With Apple Intelligence,* you can use Writing Tools to summarize selected text with a tap, proofread your work, and create different versions of the same text to help you find the right wording and tone. See Use Writing Tools with Apple Intelligence.
*Apple Intelligence is available in beta with support for the following languages: English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Japanese, Korean, and Vietnamese. Some features may not be available in all languages or regions.
