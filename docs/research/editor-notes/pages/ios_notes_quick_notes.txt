			Use Quick Notes on iPhone
You can use Quick Notes to jot down information over any app or screen on iPhone. Add links, images, tags, and mentions to a Quick Note so you can get to important names, numbers, and ideas easily. When you highlight text in Safari or add a link from an app, you see a Quick Note thumbnail next time you visit the site, taking you right to what you were viewing before.
 [IMG: A Quick Note showing a link to a location in Maps and text underneath. The Save button is on the top left.] You can access all your Quick Notes in the Notes app. And when you make a Quick Note on your iPhone, you can see the note on your iPad and Mac, too.
Take a Quick Note
To make a Quick Note, do any of the following:
From any app or screen: Add  [IMG: the Quick Note button]  to Control Center, then tap  [IMG: the Quick Note button]  in Control Center. Tap Save when you’re done.
From supported apps: Tap  [IMG: the Share button] , then tap Add to Quick Note. Tap Save when you’re done.
View and organize Quick Notes
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
Tap Quick Notes in the folders list. You may have to tap  [IMG: the Back button]  (or  [IMG: the Show Sidebar button] ) to see your folders.
Note: You can’t lock a Quick Note unless you move it to a different folder.
If you move an individual Quick Note to a different folder, it becomes a standard note and no longer appears as a Quick Note in other apps.
