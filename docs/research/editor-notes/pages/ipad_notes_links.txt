			Add links in Notes on iPad
In the Notes app, you can add links to webpages or sections in a note. You can also link to related notes, like a trip itinerary and a list of recommended restaurants. When you add street and email addresses, phone numbers, and dates, they automatically become underlined text that you can tap to take action on.
 [IMG: Adding a link to a section in a note in the Notes app.] Link to a webpage
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPad.
In the note where you want to insert a link, select the text or tap to place the insertion point.
Tap  [IMG: the Forward button] , tap Add Link, then enter the URL to specify the link destination. Optionally, you can add a name for the link.
Tap  [IMG: the Done button] .
Link to a note or section of a note
Note: To link to a section of a note, Organize a note with sections and headings.
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPad.
In the note where you want to add a link, select the text or tap to place the insertion point.
Tap  [IMG: the Forward button] , tap Add Link, then do any of the following:
Link to a section in the same note: Tap Link to Section, then select a section. To customize the link name, turn on Use Section Title or enter a custom link name.
Note: If the title of a linked note or section changes, any link that uses the note or section title as its name is updated automatically.
Link to another note: Start typing the title of the target note, then select it. To customize the link name, turn on Use Note Title or enter a custom link name.
Tip: To quickly link to another note, type “>>” in the note body, then enter the title of the target note. You can then link to a section in that note by typing “/,” then selecting a section.
Link to a section in another note: Link to another note, tap Link to Section, then select a section.
Tap  [IMG: the Done button] .
To edit or remove the link, touch and hold the link, then choose an action.
Take action on addresses, phone numbers, dates, and more
In your notes, an underline appears below text that’s recognized as a street or email address, phone number, date, or other data. Tap the underlined text to take action on it. For example, you can see a street address in Maps, compose an email, call a phone number, add a calendar event, or convert units and currency.
Note: Data detection is available when the system language of your iPad is set to a supported language in Settings  [IMG: ]  > General > Language & Region. See the iOS and iPadOS Feature Availability website.
