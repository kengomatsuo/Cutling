			Share notes and collaborate in Notes on Mac
In Notes, you can send a copy of a note to a friend. You can also share a link to invite people to collaborate in real time on a note or on a folder of notes in iCloud, and everyone will see the latest changes.
Note: All Notes features described in this guide are available when using iCloud notes. Some features aren’t available when using accounts from other providers. You must use upgraded iCloud notes to collaborate on a shared note.
 [IMG: A Notes app window on Mac showing a list of notes and the Share menu for an opened note. Options include link sharing, Add to Reading List, AirDrop, and various apps, such as Mail, Messages, and Freeform. There are also options to copy the link, copy the note, or edit extensions.] 
Share a copy of a note
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Open the note you want to share.
Do one of the following:
From the notes list or gallery view: Control-click the note you want to share, then choose Share Note. You can also swipe left on a note using two fingers on the trackpad (one finger on the Magic Mouse), then click  [IMG: the Share button] .
From a note: Click  [IMG: the Share button]  in the toolbar.
Click the pop-up menu, then choose Send a Copy.
Note: By sharing a copy, you only send a static copy of the note—recipients can’t make changes to the note or see an updated version of the note unless you send it to them again.
Collaborate on a note or folder of notes using iCloud
To collaborate with others, you can share a link to a note or a folder of notes in iCloud. You can set who can access what you share and set editing permissions. See Manage shared notes and folders.
Important: Notes that you add to a shared folder are automatically shared with anyone who shares the folder. Before you add a note to a shared folder, make sure the contents of the note are intended for everyone who shares the folder. You can’t collaborate on a locked note or on a folder with locked notes.
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Do one of the following:
Collaborate on a note: Open the note you want to share, then click  [IMG: the Share button]  in the toolbar. Click the pop-up menu, then choose Share a Link.
 [IMG: A note that’s been shared with others, with the Shared icon to the left of the note’s name.] Tip: If you share the note in Messages, you get updates in the Messages conversation whenever someone makes a change to the shared note.
Collaborate on a folder: Control-click the folder you want to share, then choose Share Folder.
You can also click  [IMG: the More button]  next to the folder in the sidebar, then choose Share Folder.
To change the access and permissions, click “People you choose can edit,” then set any of the following:
Who can access: Choose “People you choose” or “Anyone with the link.” With “People you choose,” you can turn on Access Requests.
Note: Requesting access to a note sends an email to the address associated with the owner’s Apple Account.
Permissions: Choose “Can edit” or “Can view and download.”
To open and edit a shared note, do either of the following:
If you shared the note: Select the note on your Mac or on iCloud.com.
If the note was shared with you by someone else: Click the link you received, then click Open.
Note: For security, you can only open a note shared by someone else if the email address or phone number used to send you the invitation is associated with your Apple Account. If that isn’t the case, you’re asked to add the email address or phone number to your Apple Account. See Verify your email address or phone number to open a shared item. After you open the note, it remains in your list of notes.
To mention a participant and notify them of important updates, type an @ sign followed by their name. You can only view notes with mentions on devices that have iOS 14.5, iPadOS 14.5, or macOS 11.3 or later. If you or another participant adds a mention to an iCloud note, that note is hidden for both you and the participant on any device with an earlier version of iOS, iPadOS, or macOS. It is also not viewable on iCloud.com. See Why notes are hidden on some devices.
View activity in a shared note
You can see details of who made changes in a shared note. All participants who have the shared note open can see the changes you and others make in near real time. If you don’t have the note open when someone makes a change to it, a small yellow dot appears to the left of the note in the notes list or gallery view to let you know there unread changes.
Participants who have two-factor authentication enabled and iCloud Keychain turned on in System Settings can see others’ changes immediately, and color-coded triangles show where others are working in the note. See Set up iCloud Keychain to autofill information.
 [IMG: A note showing notes from a meeting. Changes from participants are highlighted.] Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Click the shared note, then swipe right with two fingers over text in the note.
You can also click  [IMG: the Share button]  or participant contact images in the toolbar, then choose Show Highlights.
The name of each participant, and the time they last edited the note, appears on the left, color-coded to match the text they changed.
Tip: You can see a summary of updates since the last time you viewed the note and a day-to-day list of activity for each participant. Click  [IMG: the Share button]  or participant contact images in the toolbar, then choose Show All Activity.
View activity in a folder
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Do one of the following:
Control-click the folder you want to share, then choose Show Folder Activity.
Click  [IMG: the More button]  next to the folder in the sidebar, then choose Show Folder Activity.
