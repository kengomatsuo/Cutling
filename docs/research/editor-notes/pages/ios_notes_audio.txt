			Record and transcribe audio in Notes on iPhone
In the Notes app, you can record audio and have the spoken words transcribed to text. You can search the transcript, add the text to the note, or copy the text to other documents.
Note: Audio transcription is available on iPhone 12 and later when the device language is set to English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
Record audio and see the live transcript
You can record audio and have the spoken words transcribed in a note.
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
In a note, tap  [IMG: the Attachments button] , then tap Record Audio.
To start the recording, tap  [IMG: the Record button] .
Tip: To take notes while recording, swipe down from the top of the screen.
Do any of the following:
Pause recording: Tap  [IMG: the Pause button] .
Resume recording: Tap  [IMG: the Resume button] .
See the live transcript: Tap  [IMG: the Transcription button] .
Note: Spoken words in supported languages are automatically transcribed. If you don’t see  [IMG: the Transcription button] , your iPhone either doesn’t meet the minimum requirements or doesn’t detect a supported language.
When you’re finished recording, tap  [IMG: the Pause button] , then tap  [IMG: the Done button] .
Play an audio recording and review the transcript
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
In a note with an audio recording, tap  [IMG: the Play button]  to play it.
To review the transcript, tap the audio recording, then tap  [IMG: the Transcription button] .
Note: Spoken words in supported languages are automatically transcribed. If you don’t see  [IMG: the Transcription button] , your iPhone doesn’t meet the minimum requirements or doesn’t detect a supported language.
While viewing the transcript, you can do any of the following:
Play the audio at a specific point: Tap the text you want to hear.
Search the transcript: Tap  [IMG: the More button] , then tap Find in Transcript.
Copy the transcript: Tap  [IMG: the More button] , then choose Add Transcript to Note or Copy Transcript.
Name your recording: Tap the boldfaced title, type a name for your recording, then tap Save.
Save or delete the audio recording: Tap  [IMG: the More button] , then choose an option.
Note: Deleting the audio recording also deletes the transcript.
View a summary of an audio recording
You can use Apple Intelligence to generate a summary of the transcript, so you can get to the most important information at a glance. You can also copy and share summaries.
Note: Apple Intelligence on iOS 27 or later is available on an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or iPhone 16 model or later. Apple Intelligence is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.* Some languages that support Apple Intelligence summaries are not available for audio transcription.
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your iPhone.
Open a note with a recording.
Tap the recording, then tap Summary.
Tip: You can copy or share a summary. Tap  [IMG: the More button]  at the top of the summary, then choose an option.
