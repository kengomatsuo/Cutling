			Create and edit notes on Mac
In Notes on your Mac, you can create and edit notes, add drawings, and copy and paste text. You can also create copies of notes, or copy and paste text from a note.
Note: All Notes features described in this guide are available when using iCloud notes. Some features aren’t available when using accounts from other providers.
Open Notes for me
Create a new note
Siri: Ask Siri something like, “Create a note.” Learn how to use Siri.
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
In the sidebar, click the folder where you want to put the note.
Note: If you don’t see the sidebar, choose View > Show Sidebar.
Click  [IMG: the New Note button]  in the toolbar (or use the Touch Bar).
Type your note.
The first line of the note becomes the note’s title. You can change how the first line is formatted in Notes settings.
Your note saves automatically as you work.
Tip: You can choose dictionaries for specific languages, see suggested spellings for words, or add frequently used words or terms to a dictionary. See Get typing suggestions and correct mistakes. You can also translate text in a note.
Edit a note
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Open a note you want to edit or search your notes to find it quickly.
Make your changes.
Changes save automatically as you work.
Tip: When you type specific data (such as dates, times, locations, or phone numbers), you can easily add that information to another app. For example, Control-click a time, then choose Create Event to create a calendar event or Create Reminder to create a reminder. See Detect dates, contacts, and more in documents.
Add drawings to a note
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
In a note, click  [IMG: the Markup button]  to open the Markup palette.
Start drawing, or do any of the following:
Select a tool: Click  [IMG: the Choose a Drawing Tool button]  in the Markup palette at the bottom of the window, then choose a tool such as Fountain Pen or Watercolor.
Change a tool’s size: Choose a nib size at the top of the menu. For the Reed tool, you can also choose an angle.
Change a tool’s color or opacity: Click  [IMG: the Choose a color button] , then choose a color. To change the opacity of your strokes or the fill, click Show Colors, then adjust the slider.
Select and edit your drawing: Click  [IMG: the Selection tool button] , then double-click a stroke or drag around strokes to select them. Drag to move the selected strokes, or Control-click for more options such as Duplicate, Merge, and Separate.
Remove strokes from a drawing: Click  [IMG: the Choose a Drawing Tool button]  in the tools menu, choose Eraser, then click Pixel at the top of the menu to erase part of a stroke, or Object to erase one stroke at a time.
Recolor strokes: Click  [IMG: the Select Drawing button] , select strokes, then click  [IMG: the Choose a color button] .
Resize a drawing: Click  [IMG: the Select Drawing button] , then drag a blue dot.
Add shapes, lines, and arrows: Click  [IMG: the Add a shape button] ; click a shape, line, or arrow; then drag it where you want. To resize it, use the blue handles. If it has green handles, use them to alter the shape.
Add text boxes: Click  [IMG: the Add a text box button] , type your text, then drag the text box where you want.
Add your signature: Click  [IMG: the Add a signature button] . If signatures are listed, click one, then drag it where you want. To resize it, use the blue handles. To create a new signature, see [mh-Create and use signatures].
When you’re done drawing, click  [IMG: the Markup button]  again to close the Markup palette.
Create a copy of a note
Go to the Notes app  [IMG: ]  on your Mac.
Select the note from the list, then choose File > Duplicate Note (or Command-D).
Note: If you duplicate a shared note, the copy isn’t shared. You can’t create a copy of a locked note.
Use Writing Tools in Notes
With Apple Intelligence, you can use Writing Tools to summarize selected text with a click, proofread your work, and create different versions of the same text to help you find the right wording and tone. See Use Writing Tools to write, improve, and summarize content.
Note: Apple Intelligence on macOS 27 is not available in all languages or regions. Usage limits may apply.*
*Apple Intelligence is available with Siri and device language set to Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, or Vietnamese. Some features may not be available in all regions or languages. Some devices may not be available in all regions. Siri AI is available in beta in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 and requires an Apple Intelligence–enabled device set to a supported language. Available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish to start. Siri AI will not be initially available in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS. Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Certain Apple Intelligence features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts. Daily limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. Use of these features is subject to the Apple Intelligence Terms and Conditions. Learn more at support.apple.com/148587.
