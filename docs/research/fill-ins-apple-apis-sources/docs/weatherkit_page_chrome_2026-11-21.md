Available in iOS 18, iPadOS 18, macOS 15, watchOS 11, tvOS 18, and visionOS 2. (What's new: cloud cover by layer, comparisons to historical averages, daily snowfall and visibility, historical averages by month and day of year.)
WeatherKit requires iOS 16, iPadOS 16, macOS 13, tvOS 16, visionOS 1.0, or watchOS 9 or later.
Access to WeatherKit is included in the Apple Developer Program.
WeatherKit provides up to 500,000 API calls a month per Apple Developer Program membership.
Tiers: 500,000 calls/month included; 1 million US$49.99; 2 million US$99.99; 5 million US$249.99; 10 million US$499.99 ...
Attribution: If your apps ... display any weather data from Apple ... you must clearly display the Apple Weather trademark (Weather), as well as the legal link to other data sources.
Alerts must carry the Apple alert-details link and the issuing agency name and must not be altered.
