# What rivals' App Store previews show (read 2026-11-22)

Card ASG163. Source: the live US App Store pages (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`), whose server data lists
each preview's HLS file; the full files were downloaded and sheeted at one frame every 2.5 s (`rivals/<slug>.png`,
labelled sheet `rivals/rival-previews.png`). Search results and page scans: `rivals/search.json`, `rivals/videos.json`.

## How many carry previews

The iTunes Search API was asked for "clipboard", "clipboard manager", "clipboard keyboard", "paste keyboard", "text
snippets", "text expander", "snippets keyboard", "copy paste", "quick replies keyboard" and "saved replies" (iPhone and
Mac, 15 results each). Of about 140 rival pages, 18 carry a preview. The biggest rivals carry none: AutoPaste
(id1571506312, 125,866 ratings), Auto Paste Keyboard (id1570011952, 90,631), Paste Keyboard (id1066723174, 44,421),
WordBoard (id960167417, 5,626), Paste (id967805235, 1,357), PastePal (id1503446680). The largest with one is Clipboard -
Paste Keyboard (id1514741180, 1,876 ratings).

## What they show

None of them promotes only its newest feature. Each preview shows what the app is for, as a whole:

| App (id) | What the preview shows |
|---|---|
| Fast Reply (6448081847) | "Write a reply once", "Leave blanks for what changes", "Open it from the keyboard", "Fill in the blanks", "Insert. Done." The fill-in-the-blank reply is this app's own pitch. |
| ClipboardKeyboard (6764233931) | Numbered steps over a chat: tap the globe key, hold it and pick the keyboard, tap any saved text, pasted in any input box. Second preview: select text, save it, paste from history. |

Well-known apps that update often do the same: Things 3 (904237743) records projects, adding a to-do and Quick Find;
Fantastical (718043190) tours natural-language input, tasks, weather, widgets and scheduling; Structured (1499198946)
and Todoist (572688855) open on who the app is for and then show the whole app. Bear, Drafts, Craft, Day One, Flighty,
Halide, Notion, Raycast, Grammarly, Gboard, 1Blocker, Spark, Obsidian and Goodnotes carry no preview on their US page.

## Apple's own guidance

developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/: "your first preview might share an overview of your app experience, and
then use subsequent previews to highlight additional content or features. If you have multiple previews, make sure
that each video shows people something new about your app."

## What this means for 2.0

The first preview is watched by people who have never heard of Cutling, so it has to say what Cutling is and show its
everyday job; what 2.0 adds belongs inside that story or in the second and third previews. The six first directions
(directions.md) each lead with a 2.0 feature, and direction A's idea and words ("write it once", a blank filled in per
person) are the pitch Fast Reply already runs.
