# Brief: a range of directions for Cutling 2.0's in-app event

card: ASG152c (board ~/Repositories/undirect/docs/plans/app-store-growth/BOARD.md; the session cutling-2-release owns the card and commits)

## Goal

Directions for Cutling 2.0's App Store In-App Event: for each, the idea, the picture (the event card,
landscape, and the details page, portrait) and the words a buyer reads on it (an event name, one short
line on the card, a longer line on the details page). Each direction is a different idea, not a
variation of another. You decide everything else: how many, the medium, stills or a looping video,
colour, composition, tone.

## Why it matters

The owner searched the App Store on his phone and saw no picture under Cutling. Since iOS 15, people who
already have an app see only its icon row in search; the one picture they do see is the app's In-App
Event card. 2.0 is Cutling's biggest update.

A first round made three options that were all one idea: the black tag from Cutling's icon as a
photoreal studio still, with mint pills holding names (Maya, Jonas, Aiko), and words about writing a
reply once with a different name each time. The owner's answer, word for word:

> Why only names? Also can't you think of more directions? You always seem to get fixated on one thing
> once I say I liked one direction it's annoying

So this round is about range: ideas that a person would not mistake for one another, drawn from
everything 2.0 brings and from what buyers want, in whatever media fit each idea. That first round is
in `fastlane/events/2.0/options/` if you want to see what not to repeat.

## What 2.0 brings (read from the code and commits since 1.6.2)

The full commit list is `docs/aso/events/changes-since-1.6.2.txt`; the code is in `Cutling/` and
`CutlingKeyboard/`.

- **Variables.** A cutling can hold a blank filled in each time it is used ("Ask Each Time", for a name
  or an order number), a choice between options (a plan), today's date, a time, tomorrow, yesterday, a
  week from now, the text last copied, and where the cursor ends. They show as pills in the text. On
  iPhone and iPad the Cutling keyboard asks for each blank before it types, with values used before one
  tap away; a word typed first fills the first blank. On the Mac, ⌘⇧V opens a short form for the blanks
  before it pastes.
- **Writing like Notes.** A cutling's text opens as a page that works like Apple Notes: Bold, Italic,
  Underline, Strikethrough, Highlight, Monospace, Link, and bulleted, dashed and numbered lists.
- **Far more room.** 5,000 text cutlings and 500 images, up to 10,000 characters each (was 100, 25 and
  1,000): a whole email fits in one.
- **Every key types.** On the keyboard, a formatted cutling types when tapped; touching and holding it
  copies it with its formatting.
- **A remade setup guide** with a short video of the Settings steps, and a What's New sheet.

What buyers say they want, in their own words: `~/Repositories/undirect/docs/research/cutling-voice-of-customer.md`.

## Facts Apple publishes (not a style)

`docs/aso/in-app-events.md` and `~/Repositories/undirect/docs/research/in-app-event-art.md` (Apple's
overlay measured, and 23 live cards from other apps). In short: card 16:9, 1920x1080 to 3840x2160;
details 9:16, 1080x1920 to 2160x3840; png/jpg, or a looping mov/m4v/mp4 at 30 or 60 fps; Apple lays the
badge "MAJOR UPDATE", the name and a line of text over the lower part in white; Apple asks to avoid text
and logos in the media where possible, and no borders or added gradients. Event name up to 30
characters in title case; card line up to 50 and details line up to 120 in sentence case; no prices.
The event will run in 50 store languages with one picture pair unless a direction needs more.

Buyer copy, the owner's rule word for word: "Copy a buyer reads (App Store text, In-App Event
descriptions, promo text, posts) leads with what the buyer gets ... Words from the maker's side
("caught", "blocked", "trap", "layer", "detects", "new tricks") are a feature exhibition and do not
sell." Public copy never names the owner's country or city.

## What to hand back

- Every direction's card and details files at Apple's sizes in `fastlane/events/2.0/directions/<name>/`,
  with a preview of each under Apple's overlay carrying that direction's own words.
- One contact sheet of all directions side by side at the size people see them, for the owner's chat.
- Per direction, its idea in a line and its words with character counts.
