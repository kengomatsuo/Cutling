# Brief: art for Cutling 2.0's in-app event

card: ASG152 (board ~/Repositories/undirect/docs/plans/app-store-growth/BOARD.md; the session cutling-2-release owns the card and commits)

## Goal

The art of Cutling 2.0's In-App Event on the App Store: the event card (landscape) and the event details
page (portrait). You decide everything about it: the idea, the medium, stills or a looping video, colour,
composition, how many options to show. Nothing about the look is fixed in advance.

## Why it matters

The owner searched the App Store on his phone and saw no picture under Cutling. Since iOS 15, people
who already have an app see only its icon row in search results; the one picture they do see is the
app's In-App Event card. Cutling has never had an event. 2.0 is its biggest update, so this card is
what every existing Cutling owner, and every searcher, sees of it.

## The product, read from the repo

Cutling keeps snippets of text and images ("cutlings") and types them into any app from its own iPhone
and iPad keyboard; on the Mac a menu bar picker (⌘⇧V) pastes them. Paid once, no account, nothing
leaves the device except the person's own iCloud sync. Live today: 1.6.2.

What 2.0 brings (read from code and commits since 1.6.2; the full list is in
`docs/aso/events/changes-since-1.6.2.txt`):

- **Variables.** A cutling can hold blanks filled in each time it is typed ("Ask Each Time", for a
  name), a choice between options, today's date, a time, tomorrow, a week from now, the clipboard's
  text, and where the cursor lands. They show as pills in the text. On iPhone the Cutling keyboard asks
  for each blank as it types; on the Mac a short form asks before pasting. This is the most-asked-for
  gap people name against TextExpander (`~/Repositories/undirect/docs/research/cutling-voice-of-customer.md`).
- **Formatted text, written like Notes.** The cutling editor opens a page that works like Apple Notes:
  a bar of Aa, Variables, Bold, Italic, Strikethrough, Monospace and Link; underline, highlight and
  bulleted, dashed and numbered lists are being built for 2.0 now (session cutling-format, not
  recorded yet).
- **Far more room.** 5,000 text cutlings and 500 images, up to 10,000 characters each (was 100, 25 and
  1,000).
- **Every key types.** Tapping a formatted cutling on the keyboard types it; holding it copies the
  formatting.
- **A remade setup guide and a What's New sheet**, in Apple's own sheet style.

## What Apple publishes about event media (facts, not a style)

Read and measured in `~/Repositories/undirect/docs/research/in-app-event-art.md`, with 23 live event
cards described; Cutling's notes are in `docs/aso/in-app-events.md`. In short: card 16:9, 1920x1080 to
3840x2160; details 9:16, 1080x1920 to 2160x3840; `.png`/`.jpg`, or `.mov/.m4v/.mp4` video at 30 or 60
fps that loops; Apple lays the badge, event name and description over the lower part of both images in
white text with a blur; Apple writes "When possible, avoid using text or logos in your media, especially
if they include your event name or app name" and "Don't add borders or gradients to your media".

## House precedent

Undirect's 1.0.5 event art, which the owner called very impressive, was a photoreal studio still made
with the `studio-render` skill: `~/Repositories/undirect/Support/AppStore/events/1.0.5/` (card.png,
details.png, options/, render.mjs). It is one way that worked, not a template.

## What to hand back

- The finished card and details files at Apple's sizes in `fastlane/events/2.0/` (`card.png` and
  `details.png`, or the video files), plus whatever options you think the owner should see.
- For each option, a preview of the card and the details page as the App Store shows them, with Apple's
  overlay drawn over the art (badge "MAJOR UPDATE", a working event name and description; the final
  words come from a separate copywriter and are put in afterwards), so the owner can judge it at the
  size people see it.
- A few lines on the idea behind each option and which you would ship.

Do not commit, and do not edit `fastlane/compose/creative/` or `fastlane/screenshots/` (another session's
store screenshot work).
